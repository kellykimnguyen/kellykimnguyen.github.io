course demand dashboard

replica of my aso data work | built with sample data

sqlpower bitableau

problem

course demand lived in three messy places: enrollment exports, waitlists, and student surveys. deans couldn't see which classes students actually needed. i consolidated everything into clean datasets and dashboards that stakeholders could read in a minute. this is a rebuilt version with made-up numbers.

pipeline

enrollment exportwaitlist logstudent survey→sql: clean + join→power bi / tableau→1-page brief for deans

what stakeholders see

6courses ranked
3data sources joined
2courses to expand

sample data. blue = recommended to add sections. in the real project, this kind of analysis led to 2 new chemistry and data science courses for fall 2024.

the sql behind it

WITH enroll AS (
  SELECT course_id, COUNT(*) AS enrolled, MAX(seats) AS seats
  FROM enrollments WHERE term = '2024FA' GROUP BY course_id),
wait AS (
  SELECT course_id, COUNT(DISTINCT student_id) AS waitlisted
  FROM waitlists GROUP BY course_id),
survey AS (
  SELECT course_id, AVG(interest_score) AS interest
  FROM survey_clean GROUP BY course_id)
SELECT e.course_id,
  ROUND(100 * (0.6 * LEAST(w.waitlisted / e.seats, 1)
             + 0.4 * (s.interest / 5)), 1) AS demand_index
FROM enroll e
LEFT JOIN wait w   USING (course_id)
LEFT JOIN survey s USING (course_id)
ORDER BY demand_index DESC;

weights are illustrative. the index blends waitlist pressure and survey interest into one number that's easy to rank.

product analysis + prd

pm case study | course planning for transfer students

prdprioritizationwireframe

audience

transfer students at a large public university. they have about two years to finish a major, so every term has to count, and they're new to the campus tools.

pain points (hypotheses to validate in interviews)

  • 1. requirements are hard to map. it's unclear which course counts toward which requirement.
  • 2. waitlists are a gamble. students rarely know their odds or have a backup plan.
  • 3. planning is scattered. requirements, schedules, and seat info live in different tabs and spreadsheets.

solution

a term planner that puts the requirement checklist, a conflict-free schedule, and waitlist backups on one screen.
